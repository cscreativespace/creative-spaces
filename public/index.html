Who is this for?+–

Companies past a template, with one offer and proof already. Hospitality, consumer brands, professional services, architecture, wellness, retail, and studios. If you have no photos and no offer yet, we will say so.

What does a site cost?+–

Three published prices, in Australian dollars. The five-page website is $499. AI and workflow integrations are $899. Ecommerce integration is $1,299, added to the first two. No discovery fee before a number. A typical agency quote for this work is $5,000 to $10,000.

What is included?+–

Five-page website, $499: five pages designed around the offer, custom rather than a bought theme, phone tablet and desktop, an enquiry form, on-page SEO with titles, descriptions and a sitemap, content control after launch with a short training, analytics, and two weeks of tuning. AI and workflow integrations, $899, added to the website: enquiries are captured, a follow-up goes out, you are notified, AI drafts replies, forms and tools are connected, and there is a simple view of what needs a reply. Ecommerce integration, $1,299: the website and the workflows, plus a catalogue, checkout, payments, and a note when an order is placed.

Why is it so much cheaper than an agency?+–

We do not staff a new-business team, a project-management layer, or a second office. Senior people do the work, and the scope is fixed before we start. The market rate for a marketing site is $5,000 to $10,000.

How long does it take?+–

48 hours from content to creation, or it's free. Typically 2–3 weeks for most clients.

What is the process?+–

You click a package, or book a thirty-minute call. Then you send the content: the name, what you sell, a few photos, and the pages you need. From the moment that content is in, the site is designed, built, and deployed in 48 hours, or it is free. After launch you choose how close you stay. You can change the words yourself, step away, or stay in it. One click reaches the coder, design, marketing, and customer service. In the first 30 days the reply is within 2 hours. After that, 24 hours is the longest you wait.

How do we start?+–

Use Book a time. It opens Chris Smale's calendar on the page for a thirty-minute call. If we are a fit, you get a fixed price and a start date. You can also pay the published price on the package.

Can I pay by card?+–

Yes. Each package has a Pay button. It opens Stripe for the published price in Australian dollars. The five-page website is $499, AI and workflow integrations are $899, and the ecommerce integration is $1,299. Stripe takes the card and emails the invoice. Work starts after payment.

Can our team edit it?+–

Yes. You get content control after launch, a short training session, and a one-page guide. Most clients publish their own news and case studies the week after launch. No ticket to change a headline.

Do you write the copy?+–

We shape the structure and the key lines. Full copywriting is an add-on if you do not have a writer.

What happens after launch?+–

Two weeks of included tuning. After that, a monthly care plan — or nothing. We do not lock the site to us.

Who do I talk to?+–

The people who design the site. Book a time on the contact page. There is no account manager between you and the work.

Is this a template with our logo on it?+–

No. We design from the offer outward. Shared components inside your project, never a marketplace theme.
