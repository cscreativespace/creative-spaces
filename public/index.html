Who is this for?+–

Companies past a template, with one offer and proof already. Hospitality, consumer brands, professional services, architecture, wellness, retail, and studios. If you have no photos and no offer yet, we will say so.

What does a site cost?+–

Three published prices, in Australian dollars. The five-page website is $499. AI and workflow integrations are $899. Ecommerce integration is $1,299, added to the first two. No discovery fee before a number. A typical agency quote for this work is $5,000 to $10,000.

What is included?+–

Five-page website, $499: up to five professionally designed pages, mobile, tablet and desktop optimisation, on-page SEO, page titles and meta descriptions, and a search-friendly structure. It is a complete small-business website, built around the offer and handed over in full. AI and workflow integrations, $899: enquiry capture and follow-up automation, AI assistants and drafting tools, connected forms, tools and notifications, and dashboards and reporting. Ecommerce integration, $1,299: added to the first two, with a product catalogue, checkout, payments, and order notifications.

Why is it so much cheaper than an agency?+–

We do not staff a new-business team, a project-management layer, or a second office. Senior people do the work, and the scope is fixed before we start. The market rate for a marketing site is $5,000 to $10,000.

How long does it take?+–

Three to five weeks from kickoff to launch. Once the content is received and final, the build is 48 hours.

What is the process?+–

Four steps. A thirty-minute call — you leave with a price, or a no. Then we frame the offer and you approve the map. Design happens in the browser, two rounds, then the build. Hand-off is launch, training, and two weeks of tuning.

How do we start?+–

Use Book a time. Pick a weekday, thirty minutes, and leave your name and email. The call is on Teams. If we are a fit, you get a fixed price and a start date. You can also pay the published price on the package.

Can I pay by card?+–

Yes. Each package has a Pay button. The charge is the published price in Australian dollars, taken by Stripe. The five-page website is $499, AI and workflow integrations are $899, and the ecommerce integration is $1,299. If card payment is not switched on, use Build my site and we send a link.

Can our team edit it?+–

Yes. You get a CMS, a short training session, and a one-page guide. Most clients publish their own news and case studies the week after launch. No ticket to change a headline.

Do you write the copy?+–

We shape the structure and the key lines. Full copywriting is an add-on if you do not have a writer.

What happens after launch?+–

Two weeks of included tuning. After that, a monthly care plan — or nothing. We do not lock the site to us.

Who do I talk to?+–

The people who design the site. There is no account manager between you and the work.

Is this a template with our logo on it?+–

No. We design from the offer outward. Shared components inside your project, never a marketplace theme.
