This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use our website, book a call, buy a package, or run a free website health check.

2. What we collect

Depending on how you contact us, we may collect:

  • name and business name
  • email address and phone number
  • website URL and public page details you ask us to check
  • messages you send us, call notes and project content you provide
  • payment-related records (handled by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers)
  • basic technical logs such as approximate location derived from IP, browser type and pages visited, where our hosting or analytics tools provide them

For the free health check form we ask for website URL, business name, email and consent to follow up about your results.

3. Why we collect it

We use personal information to:

  • run the health check and show or email you results
  • follow up about those results when you consent
  • book calls, quote and deliver website or integration work
  • process payments and keep business records
  • improve the site and keep it secure
  • meet legal and accounting duties

We will not add you to a marketing list automatically from the health-check form. Any wider marketing email would only happen if you clearly opt in, and you can opt out any time.

4. Who we share it with

We use service providers to operate the business. They only get what they need to do their job. Typical providers include:

  • hosting and storage (for example Vercel and related storage)
  • payment processing (Stripe)
  • scheduling (Calendly)
  • email delivery and inbox tools
  • speed and analysis tools used in the health check (for example Google PageSpeed Insights where enabled)
  • optional AI tools used to generate parts of a report

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect rights, safety or property.

5. Storage and security

Information is stored with reputable cloud providers and in our business systems. We take reasonable steps to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Health-check submissions and reports may be stored so we can send you a PDF, improve the tool, and follow up when you have consented.

6. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, including project delivery, follow-up you consented to, and legal or tax record-keeping. You can ask us to delete information that we no longer need to keep.

7. Access and correction

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. Email csmale01@outlook.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Overseas disclosure

Some providers store or process data outside Australia. By using our site or services you understand that may happen. We choose established providers and expect them to protect information appropriately.

9. Children

Our services are aimed at businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
